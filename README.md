# Quantum-Optic-communication
This is my college paper on quantum-optic communication

This project explores the synergies between optical networks and quantum processing, highlighting the key aspects of their integration. Beginning with a brief overview of optical networks and an introduction to quantum processing, the research emphasizes the need for synergy between these advanced technologies. The focus of this project is on the synergies between the two technologies, the core of quantum optical communication optical-routing and the potential benefits of integrating these two advanced technologies. Relevant examples will be discussed to contribute to a comprehensive understanding of this evolving field.
